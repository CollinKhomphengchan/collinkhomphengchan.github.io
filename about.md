My name is **Collin Khomphengchan**, and I’m a Pre-Pharmacy student at Purdue University. I’m deeply interested in **CRISPR, gene editing, molecular genetics, and biotechnology**. I enjoy building tools that make healthcare more accessible and easier to navigate for real communities.

Growing up in northern Indiana, I saw how confusing it can be to find clinics, understand pricing, and access care. That inspired me to create the **Elkhart Health Finder**, a project focused on simplifying healthcare navigation for families who need clear, reliable information.

Outside academics, I enjoy swimming, volleyball, and exploring new ways technology can improve everyday life. My long term goal is to pursue advanced research in gene editing and biotech innovation.

Thanks for visiting my site — more projects coming soon!
